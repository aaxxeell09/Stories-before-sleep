Title: Pip and Luna Take Turns

Pip the clownfish held a yellow toy truck. Luna the dolphin wanted a turn.
Pip said, "I want to keep it." Luna waited sadly beside him.
Then Pip rolled the truck to Luna. "Your turn, then my turn," he said.
Luna rolled it back after her turn. Soon both friends were laughing.
They learned that taking turns lets everyone enjoy playing together.
